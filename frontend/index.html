How to play

Roll the die, get a word, draw it. That's Scrawdle.

A party, in the same room

  1. On a TV or laptop, press Host a game. It shows a short code of letters.
  2. Everyone opens Scrawdle on their phone, taps Join a game, and types the code and their name.
  3. The die rolls a category, then the word. Everyone draws it on their phone before the clock runs out.
  4. The drawings show on the big screen with no names. Tap your favourite on your phone (not your own).
  5. Each vote is 100 points, and the round's top drawing gets 50 more. After the last round, the winner takes the top spot.

On your own

Daily Word is one word a day, the same for everyone. Draw it, then publish it to today's gallery, where people like their favourites and the leaderboard ranks them.

Quick draw rolls a random word whenever you like.

Your drawing saves in this browser as you go. Undo is Ctrl+Z (Cmd+Z on a Mac). Today's drawings leave the gallery at midnight.

Leave your drawing?

It's kept in My drawings, so nothing is lost.
